Thanks for playing Bible Crossword Challenge! This one is about the app fitting the phone in your hand:

• Ready for iPhone Duo — closed, it's the phone you know; open it and the day's verse and puzzle sit side by side, and in a puzzle the clue list stands next to the grid
• Controls follow the display — on the Duo they move to the side, so the board keeps the height
• Cards sit flat on the page now, the way the rest of iOS does
• Share a finished puzzle as a spoiler-free block that shows how you solved it, not what the answers were
• Close buttons live in the trailing corner everywhere, under the same thumb
• Clearer tab icons — the Library is a grid, your Profile is you
