Thanks for playing Bible Crossword Challenge! This update includes:

• Sunday Special: a big 21×21 crossword every week — pinch to zoom, and this week's puzzle is free for everyone
• Pencil marks: jot candidate letters while you think, then confirm them when you're sure
• Check or reveal exactly what you need — a single letter, a word, or the whole grid
• Memory Verses: turn a verse from a solved puzzle into one you know by heart, with practice that grows as you do
• Zen mode: hide the timer for an unhurried solve — your times are still saved
• A fairer weekly leaderboard: solves that used hints no longer post times
