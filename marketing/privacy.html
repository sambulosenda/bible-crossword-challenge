Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 June 2026

The short version

Bible Crossword Challenge is offline-first. You don't need an account, and your puzzle progress, streaks, and achievements are stored only on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email, or contacts. We use anonymous analytics and crash reporting (via Google Firebase) to understand how the app is used and to fix problems. This may include:

  • Anonymous usage events (e.g. a puzzle was opened or completed)
  • Device type, OS version, and app version
  • Crash and performance diagnostics

This data is not used to personally identify you.

Purchases

Premium is sold through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes all payments — we never receive your payment details. We only learn whether your purchase succeeded.

Third-party services

We use Google Firebase (Analytics) and Apple (App Store / StoreKit). Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Privacy
  • Apple Privacy

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. No personal data is required to use it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "last updated" date.
