The Psalm 23 Crossword

15×15 grid · Easy · Ends on Psalm 23:1

Green pastures, still waters, a table spread in the presence of foes — Psalm 23 may be the best-loved chapter in the Bible, and The Lord Is My Shepherd turns its images into clues. If you know the psalm, the answers will rise up line by line as you read the grid.

For more from David's pen there's King David and the mini The Shepherd's Psalm; The Good Shepherd carries the image into John 10. Like every puzzle, it ends on the verse itself: The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want.
