# UX and functionality audit — 2026-09-02

Walked every tab, the game, the reader, the journey map, and a fresh-install
onboarding on the iPhone 17 Pro simulator at build 2.7-wip (after today's seven
commits). Data references are the 30/45/60-day PostHog reads from the same
session. Ordered by impact, not by screen.

## Severity key

- **P1** — wrong, confusing, or costs money/trust. Fix before 2.7.
- **P2** — structural UX debt. Plan for 2.8.
- **P3** — polish. Batch when touching the screen anyway.

---

## P1

### 1. Local StoreKit file is out of sync with App Store Connect (housekeeping)
On device both paywalls read App Store Connect and agree: $12.99/year with a
7-day trial. `Products.storekit` still carries $24.99 / 3-day with no
introductory offer, so simulator runs show one price on the onboarding page
(sandbox) and another on the in-app paywall (local file). Not a user-facing
bug; it means simulator screenshots and QA don't match production. Update the
file to mirror ASC. Confirmed with the owner 2026-09-02.

### 2. Four names for the same idea
- Today: "Collections" (First Steps, The Life of David 2/9) and "Journey · Day 3 of 5 · Beginnings"
- Packs tab: "Journeys" (Beginnings, Life of David **Step 3 of 9**, Parables) and "All Collections" (First Steps…)
- Profile: "Your journey · 14 books of the Bible explored" (the book map)
- Code and analytics: packs, quests, journeys, boards

The Life of David shows **2/9** on Today and **Step 3 of 9** on Packs, five
seconds apart. A pack, a journey, a quest and a collection are the same object
to a player. Pick one word (Packs), one progress number, and rename the book
map ("Books explored").

### 3. Onboarding puzzle ignores the hardware keyboard
The game accepts typed letters (2.6 added it for iPad). The onboarding mini
does not: typing "RK" did nothing, tapping the on-screen keys worked. iPad
users with a keyboard hit this on their first screen.

### 4. Onboarding progress bar lies
Three segments. All three fill on the name page, then two more pages follow
(celebration, offer). Either five segments or hide it after the solve.

### 5. "Setup complete" on the celebration page
The player solved one clue and typed a name. Nothing was set up. "Nice solve"
was the kicker one page earlier and was right. Suggest "First solve" /
"That's the whole game." consistently.

## P2

### 6. Five tabs for two jobs
Today and Library are the app. Packs is a second list of the same puzzles.
Achievements is a flat list of 12 badges with its own tab. Profile holds
settings, stats, the book map and the developer rows. The tab bar makes the
app look larger than the daily habit it actually is.

Recommendation: **Today · Puzzles · Profile**. Puzzles = Library with a Packs
segment. Achievements become a card on Profile (they already appear as "Next
achievement" on Today). Three tabs also fixes the "where are the minis" problem
below.

### 7. Library counts only dailies
"15 of 92 solved" with the profile saying "50 solved". Minis (90) and pack
puzzles (46) are not in the Library list or its count. A player who solved 50
puzzles sees 16%. Include everything, or label it "Daily puzzles".

### 8. Achievements page has no progress for locked badges except one
Lightning Round shows "< 2:00" and an empty bar; the rest show nothing until
earned. Either every locked badge shows progress or none does. Also 14 badges
on Profile vs 12 in the memory note vs 5 visible above the fold; the count
should be one number.

### 9. Journey book map legend
Four states, two of them near-identical light blue (Explored vs Visited) and
two near-identical grey (Not yet vs No puzzles). Collapse to three:
Explored (filled), Available (outlined), None (dimmed).

### 10. Profile hero card
An avatar letter, "Friend" (the placeholder), "Premium member", and an "Edit
name" pill take a third of the screen. The name prompt exists in onboarding;
Profile can show the name as a tappable row in the stats card and drop the
avatar.

### 11. Today card copy truncation
"Day 3 of 5 — the next ste…" on the Journey card. The headline is too long for
one line at default Dynamic Type; it will be worse at larger sizes. Headline =
pack name, subtitle = "Day 3 of 5".

### 12. Share has no analytics
`ShareLink` on the completion screen fires nothing. It is the one social
action left on the screen and the only one without a number. Wrap it in a
`.simultaneousGesture` log or a custom share sheet.

## P3

### 13. Large-title screens float
Packs, Library, Achievements, Profile all draw a 34pt title ~120pt below the
status bar with nothing above it. A standard navigation bar with a large title
collapses on scroll and gives the trailing controls (Library's filter) a home.

### 14. Library thumbnails
The grid glyph per row is 9pt dots in light grey; at list size it reads as
noise. Either draw it at 32pt in accent, or drop it and let the difficulty
pill carry the row.

### 15. Onboarding puzzle "Skip for now"
It sits at the very bottom under the keyboard, in secondary grey, on a page
that is otherwise a puzzle. Good. But after the solve the same slot says
"Continue" in a black pill — the visual weight jumps. Fine as is; noting so
nobody "fixes" it into a second primary.

### 16. Verse of the Day on Today
Verse truncates at three lines with an ellipsis ("…heard his praye…"). The
reference link is there; the truncation reads as a bug. Show the whole verse
or clamp on a sentence boundary.

### 17. Streak card freeze line
"2 freezes ready to protect your streak" under the week row on Today. Nice
line, but "freezes" has never been explained on any screen a new player sees.
First mention should say what one does.

## Working well (don't touch)

- Onboarding page 1: one headline, one promise, one button.
- The onboarding mini: solve first, ask later. Data agrees (96% complete it).
- Game screen: grid, clue bar, keyboard, one Hints control. After today the
  verse is one tap away and the stall nudge picks the right offer.
- The reader: chapter, verse numbers, type size control, Close. Nothing to add.
- Completion screen after today: one hierarchy, one primary action.
- Packs tab rows: icon, name, one-line description, progress. Right shape;
  the naming (item 2) is the only problem.
