# TikTok — First 10 Posts

All clues pulled from `Bible Crossword Challenge/Resources/puzzles.json`. Every post is a screen
recording + text overlay. No face, no voiceover, no new assets required.

## The arc

Posts are ordered to teach the algorithm what you are, in the order it learns best:

| # | Format | Optimising for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Easy clue, everyone answers | Comment volume |
| 2 | Shock clue | Reach / shares |
| 3 | Silent solve | Completion rate |
| 4 | Fact reveal | Saves |
| 5 | Difficulty ladder | Watch time |
| 6 | Translation debate | Comment depth |
| 7 | Name-them-all | Participation + saves |
| 8 | Forgotten miracle | Shares |
| 9 | Verse → puzzle | Brand / follows |
| 10 | Streak + leaderboard | Installs |

Do not reorder. Post 10 is the only one with a real CTA, and it only works after 1–9 have
built an audience that already knows what you make.

## Production

**Capture:** iOS Screen Recording (Control Center) → already 9:16. Trim in Photos.
**Text:** use TikTok's native text tool, not CapCut. Native text is faster and posts using
in-app editing features get a small distribution nudge.
**Audio:** trending sound at 20–30% under everything. Never silent — silent videos get suppressed.
**Font:** TikTok's "Classic" or "Serif". Big. Assume it's watched at arm's length on a cracked screen.

**The two rules that matter more than any script above:**
1. The hook must be fully readable in 1.2 seconds. If it takes longer to read, it's too long.
2. Every video should loop cleanly. Loops double watch time for free.

## Schedule

Two weeks, 5/week, Mon–Fri. Post 1–5 week one, 6–10 week two.
Best windows for this audience: 6–8am and 8–10pm local.
Cross-post every clip to Reels and Shorts the same day — same file, ~4 minutes of extra work.

## What to measure

Ignore views for the first two weeks. Watch these instead:

- **Completion rate** — anything under 50% means the hook is too slow
- **Comments per 1k views** — this is the real signal; target 10+
- **Saves** — tells you which format to make more of
- **Follows per post** — if post 9 doesn't beat the trivia posts here, the trivia is too dry

Judge the channel at 8 weeks / 40 posts. Not before. Nothing meaningful happens in two weeks.

## Before you post

**Bio link:** `biblecrosswordchallenge.com/go?s=tiktok`

Built at `web/src/pages/go/index.astro`. It detects iOS, shows a branded card, then sends the
visitor to the App Store with a campaign token attached. Non-iOS visitors are told it's an
iPhone/iPad app instead of being dumped on a store page they can't act on.

One page serves every channel — change the `s` param per profile:

| Channel | Bio link |
|---|---|
| TikTok | `biblecrosswordchallenge.com/go?s=tiktok` |
| Instagram | `biblecrosswordchallenge.com/go?s=reels` |
| YouTube | `biblecrosswordchallenge.com/go?s=shorts` |

Installs show up split by channel in **App Store Connect → App Analytics → Acquisition →
Campaigns**. No SDK, no third-party attribution service. A link that loses its `s` param reports
as `bio` rather than being miscounted as TikTok.

Numbers lag ~24h and only count App Store page views that convert, so treat them as directional
rather than exact.
